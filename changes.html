変更点What changed since June

Everything that’s different from the app we used in June. You don’t need to read this to use it.

新New

  • A ticket for each day. The stops are in time order, so you can see what’s next.
  • Getting there. Tap a stop for directions from the one before it, how long it takes and when to leave.
  • Where we sleep. A card for each stay that you can hold up to a taxi driver.
  • All the days on one page. Tap the day counter at the top.
  • Before we go. A list of what’s still to book, in its own tab. Say “I’ll do it” to take one, and tick it off when it’s done.
  • Ring up. A number pad with one-tap buttons like Dinner, Train and Taxi. Type in dollars or yen; it starts in dollars and shows the other beside it.
  • The stamp. When you’ve sent someone money, you stamp the slip. It makes a thud, which you can turn off under You.
  • History and undo. Every change any of us makes is listed, and can be undone. You also get a few seconds to undo anything right after you do it.
  • No signal needed. The app opens offline, and what you ring up is sent when you’re back online.
  • Your picture. Pick a photo under You and it shows next to your name on all three phones.
  • Sideways. Turn your phone and the screens rearrange to fit.
  • Try another day. Under You, see how the Days tab will look on any day of the trip.

変Works differently

  • The bottom bar. Five tabs became Days, Before, Money and You, with Ring up in the middle.
  • Settling up. It’s a slip that lists who pays who. Tap a row to see how the number was worked out.
  • Uneven splits. Percent and shares are now one option called Parts, or you can type exact amounts.
  • Simplify. Still there, as a switch under the slip.
  • Reminders. Remind opens a ready-made message to send in the group chat.
  • New expenses. No more emails. Lines someone else added since you last looked are marked.
  • The map. No map tab. Each stop has directions, and the all-days page shows the shape of the trip.
  • Settings. They’re under You now: your name, e-Transfer email, picture, sound and the invite link.
  • Getting in. The invite link signs you in. There’s still no account or password.

廃Gone

  • Categories. The default kept putting things in the wrong one. The quick buttons do that job now.
  • Themes. There’s one look, and it’s already dark.
  • Comments on expenses. A line can carry a note, which is what people used comments for.
  • Repeating expenses, refunds, budgets and households. Nobody used them.
  • Choosing your own currency. Dollars and yen sit side by side everywhere.
  • Guest links. It’s just the three of us, on one invite link.
  • Dragging stops into order. They sort by time.
  • The falling petals and the countdown screen. The first ticket says how many days are left.
